Meet your organisers

Two mums, one big party

Diwali Playdate started with two Singapore mums who wanted their kids to grow up loving Deepavali as much as they did.

Rinie Gupta

Founder · Entrepreneur · Mum · Pawrent

Rinie is an entrepreneur and the founder of Yawn to Dawn, a certified newborn, baby and toddler sleep consultancy. She helps tired parents of under-5s get their nights back and co-hosts The Modern Indian Parent Podcast. At Diwali Playdate she's our resident dance master, leading the Bollywood workshop. She's the one who keeps the evening alive and flowing, and she's a full-on enthu cutlet!

@riniegupta

Sana (Upasna)

Founder · Entrepreneur · Twins mama · Pawrent

Sana is an entrepreneur and tech startup founder building SympleHost, a mum to twins and a proud pawrent. She loves bringing people together and turning big ideas into real-life celebrations. Deeply rooted in her Indian culture, she loves sharing its traditions and teaching them to the next generation. At Diwali Playdate she leads the welcome and the puja, and looks after every detail down to the very last diya.

@symplesana

Why we started

Deepavali, the way we remember it

Growing up, Deepavali meant sticky hands, new clothes, diyas on every ledge and a house full of noise. We wanted our kids in Singapore to feel that too, so we threw them a party and invited everyone.
