What's on

Diwali activities kids can touch

Hands-on Diwali crafts, games and cultural activities for kids. Booths stay open all evening, so little ones can wander, make, win and come back for more.

Knock the Raavan

Knock down the cup pyramids with soft balls and win a small prize.

Diya Painting

Decorate a tea-light holder to take home, with acrylic markers, gems and mirror stickers.

Henna & Bangle Corner

Kid-friendly mehendi tattoo stickers, applied with a sponge and water. Plus a bangle corner!

Rangoli Making

Group rangoli with stencil boards, coloured rice and other sensory materials for older kids, and soft flower-petal rangoli on floor boards for little ones under 2. Finished boards go on display.

Decorate a Puja Thali

Turn a plate into your very own puja thali with mirror stickers, pom poms, paint and markers.

Puja & Ramayan

A Ganesh–Lakshmi puja, then the story of Rama, Sita and the festival of lights, told on the big screen.

👑 2026 Best Dressed

Best Dressed

The fashion show! Every child gets their moment on the runway in their festive best, whether it's a twirling lehenga, a sharp kurta or a sparkly sherwani. Cheer them on, cameras ready, and bring your A game.

Bollywood Dance

Learn a few easy Bollywood moves together in a fun, high-energy workshop made for little feet. Then the floor opens up for a free-and-easy dance party to close the night, with parents very welcome to join in.

Chaat & Bentos

A free-flow healthy snack and drinks counter for every kid: fruit, veggie sticks, dips, papadums, juice and masala chai. Chaat and kid-friendly bentos are sold by our food vendor at an affordable S$10–15 per person.

With craft tables, games, stories, a runway and a dance floor, there's always something new to try. Kids stay engaged all evening, moving happily from one station to the next, while parents get to relax, sip chai and catch up.

Talent show

Nominate a little star

Does your child love to dance, sing, play an instrument or recite a poem? Nominate them for the 2027 talent show!

How it works

  1. Open to kids aged 1–10. Solo acts, siblings or a group of friends are all welcome. For a group, list every child's name.
  2. Keep it short and sweet: up to 2 minutes per act.
  3. Every performer gets a certificate. Please type each child's full name exactly as you'd like it printed.
  4. Need music? Tell us the song now. We'll ask for the track (MP3 or YouTube link) closer to the day.
  5. A practice video is optional but helps us plan the running order. Share a link if you have one.
  6. We'll confirm each child's slot once the 2027 date and venue are announced.

Volunteer

Run a station with us

Our volunteers make the magic happen. Pick the station you'd love to run and show us how you'd bring it to life.

How to apply

  1. Pick your station. Choose a first and second choice from our activities (see what each one involves before you choose).
  2. Record a short role-play video (1–2 minutes). A phone video is perfect. Pretend a small group of 4–6 year olds has just walked up to your station:
    • Welcome them warmly and introduce yourself.
    • Explain the activity in simple, kid-friendly words.
    • Show how it's done step by step. Props from home are great!
    • Show how you'd handle a tricky moment, like a child upset that their tower fell, a toddler who wants to grab everything, or a queue forming.
  3. Upload the video to Google Drive, YouTube (unlisted) or Dropbox, set sharing to "Anyone with the link can view", and paste the link below.
  4. Plan to arrive early. Volunteers arrive before doors open for setup and a short briefing, and help pack up at the end.
  5. Under 18? Please fill this in with a parent.

Please note: new activity ideas are reviewed for volunteer shortlisting only and may not be included in the final event.

What to do this Deepavali in Singapore with kids

Wondering what to do on the Deepavali weekend, or looking for Deepavali events in Singapore that are actually made for children? Diwali Playdate is a kid-friendly Diwali party in Singapore for families with babies, toddlers, preschoolers and kids up to 10. Little ones try Deepavali crafts like rangoli and diya painting, make their own puja thali, hear the Ramayan story, show off their Deepavali kids outfits at the best dressed fashion show, and learn a Bollywood kids dance.

It's a festive Diwali celebration in Singapore and one of the best things to do with kids around Deepavali. It's also a warm way for Indian families, mixed families and expat families to meet other parents and become part of the community. FAQ

Good to know

When do 2027 tickets go on sale?

As soon as we've secured the 2027 venue. Waitlist families hear first and get early-bird access before tickets open to everyone.

What ages is it for?

Kids aged 1 to 10. There are floor activities for babies and plenty to keep big kids busy.

Do we need to be Indian or Hindu to come?

Not at all. Deepavali is celebrated across Singapore, and every family is welcome to share in it.

Do parents come too?

Yes, this isn't a drop-off. Adults need their own ticket, and last year adult tickets cost much less than children's.

What should my child wear?

Your festive best: there's a best dressed competition! Things can get colourful, so maybe not the most precious outfit.

Is there food?

Yes! A free-flow healthy snack and drinks counter is included for all kids. A chaat counter and non-spicy Indian and Western bentos are also available to buy directly from the food vendor at the event. We keep prices affordable, around S$10–15 per person.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

All sales are final and non-refundable. As a small community event, we pay for the venue, craft supplies and food upfront, so tickets can't be cancelled or refunded once booked.

Is it suitable for toddlers and preschoolers?

Yes. We plan Deepavali activities for toddlers and preschoolers too, like flower-petal rangoli on floor boards for under-2s, simple crafts and lots of music and dancing. Babies under 1 are very welcome.

What should kids wear for Deepavali?

Any festive Deepavali kids outfit works: a kurta, lehenga, salwar or a bright party dress. There's a best dressed fashion show, so go all out if your little one loves to dress up!

When is Diwali Playdate 2027?

We hold it in the weeks around Deepavali in Singapore. The exact 2027 date and venue will be announced once confirmed, and waitlist families hear first.

Does joining the waitlist commit me to anything?

Not at all. It just means you'll be first to know the date, venue and ticket details.
