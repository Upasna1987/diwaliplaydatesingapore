What's on

A festival they can touch

Craft and game booths stay open all evening, so kids can wander, make, win and come back for more.

Knock the Raavan

Knock down the cup pyramids with soft balls and win a small prize.

Diya Painting

Decorate a tea-light holder to take home, with acrylic markers, gems and mirror stickers.

Henna Corner

Kid-friendly mehendi tattoo stickers, applied with a sponge and water. Plus a bangle corner!

Rangoli Making

Group rangoli with stencil boards and colours, with floor boards for the babies. Finished boards go on display.

Decorate a Puja Thali

Turn a plate into your very own puja thali with mirror stickers, pom poms, paint and markers.

Puja & Ramayan

A Ganesh–Lakshmi puja, then the story of Rama, Sita and the festival of lights, told on the big screen.

Best Dressed

The fashion show! Every child gets a moment on the runway in their festive best. Bring your A game.

Bollywood Dance

A dance workshop, then a free-and-easy dance party to close the night.

Book Swap & Snacks

Bring a pre-loved book, take one home. Festive snacks and drinks keep everyone going.

Why we started

Deepavali, the way we remember it

Growing up, Deepavali meant sticky hands, new clothes, diyas on every ledge and a house full of noise. We wanted our kids in Singapore to feel that too, so we threw them a party and invited everyone.

FAQ

Good to know

When do 2027 tickets go on sale?

As soon as we've secured the 2027 venue. Waitlist families hear first and get early-bird access before tickets open to everyone.

What ages is it for?

Kids aged 1 to 10. There are floor activities for babies and plenty to keep big kids busy.

Do we need to be Indian or Hindu to come?

Not at all. Deepavali is celebrated across Singapore, and every family is welcome to share in it.

Do parents come too?

Yes, this isn't a drop-off. Adults need their own ticket, and last year adult tickets cost much less than children's.

What should my child wear?

Your festive best: there's a best dressed competition! Things can get colourful, so maybe not the most precious outfit.

Does joining the waitlist commit me to anything?

Not at all. It just means you'll be first to know the date, venue and ticket details.
