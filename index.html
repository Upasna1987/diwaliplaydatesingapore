What's on

A festival they can touch

Every station is designed for small hands and short attention spans. Kids move at their own pace, and parents get to join in.

Rangoli art

Fill in bright rangoli patterns with coloured sand and petals — perfect for little hands.

Paint your own diya

Decorate a clay diya to take home and light (with a grown-up) on Deepavali night.

Mehendi & bangle corner

Kid-safe mehendi tattoos, plus a rainbow of bangles to try on.

Ramayan storytime

The story of Rama, Sita and the festival of lights, told on the big screen with lots of audience participation.

Aarti & diya lighting

Kids gather round the thali, light diyas together and learn why we celebrate.

Knock down Ravana

Our festive twist on cup-stacking — aim, throw, and watch Ravana tumble.

Desi fashion walk

Every child gets their moment on the runway in their festive best.

Bollywood dance party

A high-energy dance session that ends in giggles every time.

Festive food

Kid-friendly bento sets and chaat for the grown-ups.

Why we started

Deepavali, the way we remember it

Growing up, Deepavali meant sticky hands, new clothes, oil lamps on every ledge and a house full of noise. Far from home — or just busy with life in Singapore — it's hard to recreate that for our kids. So we built a morning that does it for you.

  • Culture first, but every family is welcome
  • Small group sizes so it never feels overwhelming
  • Parents join in — this isn't a drop-off
